Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Nacht3Studio Analiz ("the app") is a private tool operated by Mehmet Tevfik Ustaoglu for his own YouTube channel Nacht3Studio. It is not offered to the public.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube Analytics data of the owner's own channel (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, countries).
  • Public comments on the channel's own videos, in order to draft replies.

How the data is used

The data is used only to analyse the channel's performance and to reply to comments. Replies are posted only after the channel owner approves them. The app never edits or deletes videos or comments.

Storage and sharing

Data and access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data is then deleted.
